# 0032. Cost attribution, platform cost plans and contract volumes

- **Status:** Accepted (owner decision 2026-11-11: "go for it"; the volume of part 3 added by the owner the same day) and built in 0.19.0 – see _Built in 0.19.0_
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Since:** 0.19.0

## Context

Since 0.16.0 a **service** plans what it spends per fiscal year ([ADR 0026](0026-service-cost-planning.md)), since 0.18.0 a supplier contract carries **payment schedules** with one paying service each and Steward shows what is still owed ([ADR 0030](0030-contract-payments-and-recorded-costs.md)), and a contract has its own scope – the services it covers, owners, a visibility of its own ([ADR 0031](0031-supplier-contracts-and-contacts-scope.md)). The owner asked: _"Can contracts / costs also be attributed to applications / systems / platforms (integrations)? What makes sense?"_

What teams run into:

- **Applications and systems** are what a contract is often _for_ – a support contract for one historian host, licences for one application. Plan lines, entries and schedules can already name an application or system of the service, but a contract cannot, and nothing on the application's or system's page says what is planned, paid or contracted for it. An application used by two services is planned by both; giving it a plan of its own would count it twice.
- **Platforms** (Steward's integrations: AWS, Snowflake, Confluent, AI gateways, FOCUS files) have contracts no service holds: a data platform's capacity, a cloud spend commitment, a streaming subscription. The platform team pays them. Steward already reads each platform's bill, but there is no place to plan the platform team's own contracts and set them against that bill. ADR 0026 refused **chargeback** (allocating a platform's costs to the services that use it), and that stays.
- **Contract amounts are often a volume, not a price.** Capacity bought up front and drawn down by use; a framework budget of translations or support hours invoiced monthly by use, with a committed minimum; a not-to-exceed ceiling. ADR 0030's schedules know fixed payments only, so such a contract is planned as if it were spent in full, nobody sees how much is used, and the money lost when a minimum or a prepaid volume is not used up shows only at the end.

The boundaries of ADR 0026 and 0030 stay: lightweight, no approvals, no invoices, no currency conversion, no chargeback. Steward is not the system of record for finance.

## Options considered

1. **Plans for applications and systems** (a cost plan per application, like a service's). Rejected: an application shared by services would be planned and counted twice, and two plans of the same money disagree. Applications and systems get **attribution** – a read-only rollup of what the services name for them.
2. **Platform costs allocated to services** (showback by usage share, or a split rule per contract). Rejected, as in ADR 0026: allocation keys are a FinOps concern, they make the platform's own budget disappear into the services, and every split invites disputes. A platform plans and pays its own contracts.
3. **A platform as a "service"** (model the platform team as a service and plan there). Rejected: the platform's read costs, its owning teams and its managers would not apply; it duplicates the catalogue.
4. **A platform cost plan of its own, the service plan's features** (chosen). One model – plan lines, entries, Book, the CSV import, copy, export, proposals from schedules, commitments – with the owner being a service or a platform.
5. **Volumes as a separate entity** beside schedules (a "commitment" object with its own drawdown). Rejected in favour of a schedule **billing kind** _volume_: one place per contract for what it costs, the same proposals, commitments, rights and audit.
6. **Counting both entries and read costs as the use of a volume.** Rejected: an invoice booked and the bill read are the same money. A volume has one source of use.

## Decision

**The service plans and pays; applications and systems get attribution; a platform gets a cost plan of its own; a contract can cover platforms and name the applications and systems of the services it covers; a payment schedule can be paid by a platform and can be a volume – prepaid or billed by use, fully committed, with a minimum or a ceiling only – whose use, rest, linear estimate and warnings Steward shows, counting nothing twice.** Code: `shared/src/costContracts.ts` (the billing _volume_, volume periods and payments, proposals), new `shared/src/costVolumes.ts` (estimate, warnings, owed, prepaid drawdown – pure functions), `shared/src/costPlans.ts` (owner, attribution types), `server/src/costs/plans.ts` (an owner – service or platform – for every plan function), new `server/src/costs/volumes.ts` (the use in bulk, what a prepaid volume covers, the status), new `server/src/costs/volume-notices.ts` (event `cost.volume`), new `server/src/costs/attribution.ts`, `server/src/costs/schedules.ts`, `commitments.ts`, `entry-import.ts`, `booking-reminders.ts`, `server/src/suppliers/contract-scope.ts` and `service.ts` (platforms and items on contracts, the read and edit rules); web `web/src/components/costs/ServicePlan.tsx` (the plan of a service or a platform), new `Volumes.tsx`, `Attribution.tsx`, `PlatformContracts.tsx`, `ContractPayments.tsx` (payer and volume), `Commitments.tsx`, `web/src/components/suppliers/ContractServices.tsx` (items, covered platforms).

### 1. Applications and systems: attribution, not a plan

- **A contract names what it is for.** Per covered service, optionally, the **applications of that service and the systems its applications run on** that the contract is for (`supplier_contract_items`), at most 50 – like the purpose. None: the service as a whole. Only items the person may see can be chosen; items they may not see stay on save. Who may change the covered service's purpose changes them (ADR 0031: who changes the contract, or edits that service).
- **"Costs and contracts"** on the application's and the system's page (Costs module): the contracts that name it for a covered service, or that its lines, entries and schedules pay for; the **plan lines, cost entries and payment schedules that name it** in the services that use it – an entry names it by its own target or its line's; and **totals per fiscal year and currency** (planned from lines, incurred from entries). Each line, entry and schedule belongs to one service and is **listed and counted once**; schedules are listed, not totalled (their lines are). Read-only.
- **Team scope:** only services the reader may see (a hidden service's lines are neither listed nor counted), contracts by ADR 0031's rule (hidden ones are neither listed nor counted), amounts by the service's _Hide amounts from viewers_ – a row without amounts is listed without them and left out of the totals (`amountsHidden`).
- Also `GET /api/v1/applications/{id}/cost-attribution`, `GET /api/v1/systems/{id}/cost-attribution`.

### 2. Platforms: a cost plan of their own

- **A contract covers platforms** besides services (`supplier_contract_platforms`), each with a purpose, at most 20 per contract.
- **A schedule is paid by a service or a platform** (`cost_schedules.platform_id`; `service_id` '' then). The paying platform must be covered: a schedule for another platform adds it when the person may change the contract, else it is refused (`COST_SCHEDULE_PLATFORM_NOT_COVERED`); a platform that pays a schedule cannot leave the contract (`SUPPLIER_CONTRACT_PLATFORM_HAS_SCHEDULES`).
- **A platform cost plan per fiscal year** with everything a service plan has: lines (no application, system or platform to name – `COST_PLAN_TARGET`), entries, _Book_, the CSV import of entries, _Copy to FY …_, CSV export, proposals from its schedules and _Update lines_, prepaid rows, renewals, the opt-in booking reminder, the _Contracts_ section with its commitments. Rows of a platform carry `platform_id` with `service_id` '' (the column stays NOT NULL); a service's rows are unchanged.
- **Against the platform's read costs.** The platform's whole bill as Steward reads it is the plan's _costs read_: a line of category Cloud takes them per currency (the oldest), what no line takes is the row _the platform's costs (read)_ with the forecast for the months to come – per month planned / incurred (read plus entries) / expected / difference, as for a service. A platform's total is its bill, so its readers see it whole (as on the costs page).
- **Who:** who may manage the platform and the members of its owning teams change the plan (as for the platform's settings; `ScopeInfo.canEditPlan`); who may see the platform reads it, amounts by the existing rule (its managers and owning teams' members, administrators and auditors; others unless _Hide amounts from viewers_). Viewers see no platforms. New writes are as for services: Book, proposals, _Update lines_, the import, schedules need a session; lines and entries accept API tokens.
- **Not:** allocation or chargeback of a platform's costs to the services that use it (ADR 0026), approvals, invoices, currency conversion. A service's plan keeps showing the costs read for its applications – on a platform with prepaid capacity too; the two plans are never added up.
- Also the team rollup (a team's platforms beside its services, marked), the dataset _Cost plans_ (a row per platform plan), `GET /api/v1/platforms/{platformId}/cost-plan`, the MCP tool `get_platform_cost_plan`. The report section _Costs_ is about costs read and stays as it is.

### 3. Volumes: prepaid, by use, minimum or ceiling

A schedule of billing **volume** names an amount per **contract year** (years 2–10 with amounts of their own) or for the **whole term**, in the contract currency, and:

| Field             | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| How it is billed  | **Prepaid** – paid up front, one payment at the start of each period (the first: the first payment date), then drawn down; **by use** – billed in arrears from what is used (e.g. monthly invoices)                                                          |
| What is committed | **Full** (use it or lose it), a **minimum** (an amount, capped at the volume, or a percentage rounded half away from zero to minor units) with the rest an upper limit, or **nothing** (a framework budget, a not-to-exceed ceiling). Prepaid is always full |
| Use from          | The **cost entries booked to the contract** (any service or platform) – default for a service; or the paying **platform's read costs** – default for a platform, only for a platform. Never both                                                             |

- **The use** of a period: entries naming the contract in its currency and period – not those on lines of the contract's other schedules (fixed payments) nor on a prepaid volume's own payment line – or the platform's read costs per day. **One volume schedule per contract and currency** (`COST_VOLUME_ONE_PER_CURRENCY`), so the same entries are never the use of two volumes.
- **Shown** (the plan's _Volumes_ section, the _Contracts_ section and _Costs → Commitments_): the current period, used, left, the share used, **an estimate** of the use at the period's end – linear, the use per day over the last 90 days up to yesterday times the days left, from 28 days of the period on, labelled as an estimate everywhere – the day it runs out at that rate, and warnings: **used up** / **runs out before the period ends** (prepaid or fully committed), **over the ceiling** / **expected over the ceiling** (a minimum or nothing committed), **shortfall** (expected to end at least 10 % below the committed minimum, once a quarter of the period is over – money paid or owed for nothing).
- **Team scope of the use:** entries of every service and platform that books to the contract count. A reader who may not see all of them gets **the warnings only** (`stateOnly`) – no used, left, share, estimate or what a by-use volume owes – as for budgets: the figures would give away the others' costs by subtraction.
- **Commitments count only what is owed:** prepaid – its payments dated today or later (as any schedule); by use – per period not over, the **committed minimum not used yet** (a period still to come: its whole minimum), counted in the fiscal year the period ends in; a ceiling – nothing. What a volume may still bill beyond that is **"up to …"** (`upTo`), shown apart and **never added to what is owed**, also in the totals.
- **Plan lines:** proposed per fiscal year like any schedule – prepaid: the payment as a one-off line in its month (prepaid rows in the years it covers); by use: per period a yearly line over its months in the year, the volume spread evenly over all the period's months in minor units and summed per year, so the lines of all years add up to the volume exactly. Editable like other proposed lines; _Contract changed_ and _Update lines_ as in ADR 0030.
- **Incurred:** a line by use is projected month by month; its incurred is its entries (use from entries) or the platform's read costs of its months (use from read costs – the line takes them before any cloud line; it is not booked, `COST_BOOK_READ`). The difference shows under- or over-use.
- **Nothing counted twice.** Use drawn from a **prepaid** volume – read costs of the platform, or entries naming the contract – is covered by the payment: in every cost plan that part (date order, up to the volume) is not counted as incurred again but shown as _drawn from prepaid volumes_ (`drawn` per entry and per currency); what is past the volume is an overage and counts. By-use volumes are cash when billed: their entries and read costs count as usual.
- **Book** on a line billed by use asks for the actual amount (`COST_BOOK_AMOUNT_REQUIRED`) and suggests the average use per month so far in the period.
- **Notification** `cost.volume` (_Contract volume_, in the app and by e-mail by default like _Cost budget_; quiet-hours group _Budgets and costs_): once per schedule, period and kind (`cost_volume_notices`) – used up, over the ceiling and expected over it at once, _runs out_ when the estimated day is within 30 days, a shortfall when the period ends within 90 days – to the payer's owners who see its amounts (a service's named owners and owning teams' members; a platform's managers and owning teams' members), else the administrators. Each recipient reads the figures only when they see the payer's amounts and every service and platform that books to the volume, and the contract's title only when they may see the contract. Checked at most hourly by the job _costs_.

### 4. Who sees a contract covering a platform

ADR 0031's rule, extended: a contract with a team among its owners is seen by its owners, the readers of a service it covers, administrators, auditors – and **who manages a platform it covers or is a member of that platform's owning teams**. **Not every reader of the platform:** platforms are shared by many teams that only use them, and the platform team's capacity contract, its terms and amounts are the platform team's (`SupplierContractReadReason` `platform`). A contract without a team owner stays visible to the supplier's readers.

- **Changing the contract:** as ADR 0031, and who manages a covered platform counts like an editor of a covered service – they change the contract when they also see every service and every platform it covers.
- **Adding a covered platform:** who may change the contract and may see the platform; **the purpose and taking it off:** who may change the contract, or who manages that platform. Covered platforms the reader may not see are counted for the owners and who may change it (`hiddenPlatforms`), never named. Signed-in session only; audited on the supplier (`supplier.contract_platform_added|changed|removed`), with `teamOwned` on adding.
- **In the platform's plan**, a schedule of a contract the reader may not see shows as _a contract of a supplier you may not see_ – amounts by the plan's rule, no supplier, title or dates (ADR 0030).
- **Elsewhere:** `/api/v1/suppliers/{id}` lists `platforms` per contract (those the token owner may see, no count), `services[].items`; `GET /api/platforms/:id/contracts` lists the contracts covering a platform the reader may see; _Replaced by…_ moves covered platforms the person may see and the items of the services that join; removing a contract removes its platforms and items; deleting a platform removes its own cost plan, its schedules and its place on contracts (counts in `platform.deleted`).

### Data model (additive migrations)

- `costs-005-platform-plans-volumes`: `platform_id` on `cost_plan_lines`, `cost_entries`, `cost_schedules`, `cost_entry_imports` (indexes); `cost_schedules.volume_per`, `volume_billing`, `volume_committed`, `volume_minimum` (micros), `volume_minimum_pct`, `volume_consumption`; an index on `cost_entries(supplier_contract_id, entry_date)`; `cost_volume_notices` (schedule, period start, kind – send bookkeeping, not moved). `cost_booking_reminders.service_id` holds the plan's owner, a service or a platform.
- `suppliers-005-contract-platforms-items`: `supplier_contract_platforms` (contract, platform, purpose, added by and at), `supplier_contract_items` (contract, service, kind, item, added by and at; index on kind and item). Person columns are history.

### Audit, interfaces, limits

- Plan, entry, schedule and import actions of ADR 0026/0030 on a platform's plan have `entityType: platform`. New: `supplier.contract_platform_added|changed|removed`; `supplier.contract_service_changed` also records `items`. Schedule snapshots include the volume.
- Internal: `/api/costs/platform-plans/:platformId[/…]` (the routes of `/api/costs/plans/:serviceId`), `/api/costs/schedules?platformId=`, `/api/costs/commitments?platformId=`, `/api/costs/attribution/:kind/:id?fiscalYear=`, `PUT|DELETE /api/suppliers/:id/contracts/:contractId/platforms/:platformId`, `GET /api/platforms/:id/contracts`, `GET /api/services/:id/contract-items`; schedules take `platformId` or `serviceId` and `volume`; contracts take `platforms` (on create) and `services[].items`.
- Public, read-only: `GET /api/v1/platforms/{platformId}/cost-plan`, `GET /api/v1/applications/{id}/cost-attribution`, `GET /api/v1/systems/{id}/cost-attribution`; `CostPlanYear` gains `owner`, `volumes`, `drawn`, lines' `volume`; `CostCommitment` gains `payer`, `upTo`, `volume`; `/api/v1/cost-commitments?platformId=`. MCP: `get_platform_cost_plan`; `list_cost_commitments` takes `platformId`. Datasets: `costs.plans` a row per platform plan (column _Platform_); `costs.commitments` columns _Platform_ and _Up to_. The export part's `commitments.csv` gains platform and volume columns at the end.
- Limits: 20 covered platforms per contract, 50 items per covered service; a platform's plan has the service plan's limits (200 lines, 2,000 entries per fiscal year, 200 schedules); one volume per contract and currency.

## Consequences

- An application or system shows what its services plan, pay and hold contracts for, without a second plan and without counting a shared application twice.
- A platform team plans its own contracts against the bill Steward reads, in the same place and with the same tools as a service team, and Costs → Commitments and a team's rollup include them.
- A volume shows early whether it will run out or be wasted, and what is owed is never inflated by a ceiling. Prepaid capacity is counted once – the payment – and its drawdown is visible.
- More concepts: the payer, volumes, _up to_, drawn amounts, state only. The forms keep volume fields to the billing _volume_; the docs explain them with the demo.
- **Risks:** a linear estimate is wrong when the use changes – labelled as an estimate, from 28 days on, with its window; a reader confused by state only – said in the panel; a platform's plan and a service's plan both showing the same read costs – they are never added up, and chargeback stays out; a contract covering a platform widens who sees it to the platform team – a person's decision in a session, audited, only who may change the contract.
- Revisit if teams need allocation keys after all, several volumes per contract and currency, per-tranche expiry inside a prepaid term, or currency conversion.

## Built in 0.19.0

Built as decided. Settled while building:

- **The owner of a plan** is a pair (kind, id); a platform's rows keep `service_id` '' because the column is NOT NULL since 0.16 and an additive migration cannot change that. Service code paths that join `services` leave platform rows out on their own.
- **One plan view for both owners** (`planYearOf`) – a fixed number of statements whatever the number of lines and entries, also for a platform (a query-count test like the service plan's); volumes read their use in bulk (one statement for the entries of all contracts involved, one for the lines to leave out, one for read costs).
- **Volume periods per contract year cut at the schedule's end**; a term without an end (automatic renewal, no end date) runs at most ten years, like other schedules.
- **What a by-use volume owes** is counted in the fiscal year the period ends in (a minimum is settled at the end of the period); prepaid volumes reuse the payment arithmetic of ADR 0030.
- **Prepaid drawdown** goes in date order (then by entry id or day); credits are not drawn. Read costs are drawn per day of their charge period and shown per month.
- **The notice job** runs at most hourly in the job _costs_, after the vault is unlocked (nothing claimed while e-mail cannot be sent).
- **Contracts covering a platform** are added without the `access.grant` step-up, like covered services in ADR 0031: the person may change the contract and see the platform, it needs a session and is audited. Recorded here as an open point to revisit if the security review asks for a step-up.
- **Platform deletion** removes the platform's plan, schedules and coverage in its transaction (counts in the audit entry) rather than refusing the deletion.
- **The 0.19 UX review** findings in these files were fixed with it: the commitments table puts _to contract end_, _to earliest exit_ and _notice by_ first with their dates as a second line (no tooltip), labels the phone card's notice date and folds schedules that owe nothing behind _Show paid schedules_ (M9); the filter wording and _All_ placeholders (L13); the application's AWS read-out uses the currency's formatting (L7); the dataset metrics round to the currency's minor units and the unused `microsToCents` is gone (L8); the CSV import's file placeholder (L16); _Costs_ links each service, platform and team to its cost plan (L17); German glossary terms (L14).
